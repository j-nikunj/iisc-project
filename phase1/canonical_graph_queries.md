Goal: Define the key questions GraphRAG should answer over the Bengaluru seed graph.

1) Which corridors are most impacted by congestion zones and infrastructure failures?
2) Which agencies own assets within the ORR IT Corridor region?
3) What maintenance events are constraining BMTC services this quarter?
4) Which flooding zones overlap the Outer Ring Road corridor?
5) Which transit services depend on assets with low condition index?
6) What policies regulate BMTC and BMRCL operations?
7) Which stakeholders are most impacted by Silk Board congestion?
8) What sensors monitor the highest severity congestion zones?
9) Which assets are maintained by BBMP but funded by state grants?
10) Which congestion zones correlate with peak-hour IT commute patterns?
11) Which corridors connect Whitefield Region to CBD regions?
12) What is the shortest dependency chain from a policy to a congestion zone?
13) Which operators are constrained by flooding zones during monsoon?
14) Which urban regions have the highest concentration of infrastructure failures?
15) Which transit services serve both ORR IT Corridor and Whitefield Region?
16) Which funding mechanisms support metro services versus bus services?
17) Which congestion zones overlap with critical transit transfer points?
18) Which assets are unmonitored by any sensor system?
19) Which agencies regulate operators that serve airport commute patterns?
20) Which maintenance events can reduce congestion in Silk Board zone?
21) Which corridors have the highest count of overlapping zones (congestion + flooding)?
22) Which policies constrain freight movement patterns in urban core zones?
23) What are the causal chains from infrastructure failure to stakeholder impact in ORR?
24) Which operators depend on corridors with repeated maintenance events?
25) Which zones are served by multiple transit services but still show high congestion severity?
